# Bigger maps and map typologies (milestone 9b) — decisions

Settled with the owner in one round. Builds on milestone 9a (roles and compositions).

| Decision | Choice | Why | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Map size | Grows with the tower, then **stops**: floors 1–9 keep 9–11 cells, 10–39 get 12–15, **from 40 on 15–18 for ever**; boss floors and stages +2 (at most 20). Deeper floors get harder through levels, teams and harsher typologies, never through bigger boards | Huge maps would be a pain to play (owner) | playtests |
| Approach | Enemies spawn a **bounded walking distance** from the start zone (about 6–9 MP), whatever the size: first contact within a turn or two; the extra room is for manoeuvring (flanks, high ground, chokepoints), not a longer walk | Keeps big boards quick | — |
| Team size | Unchanged (9a's compositions, 2 to 5) | Balance comes later (owner) | the balance milestone |
| Typologies | Six: **open field** (today's plateaus), **mountain** (a central peak, slopes and ridges), **crater** (a high rim around a low centre), **islands and bridges** (raised islands over holes, one-cell bridges as chokepoints), **canyon / river** (a hole or low corridor across the map with 2–3 crossings), **ruins** (obstacle walls forming rooms and corridors). From noise (`FastNoiseLite`) seeded by the floor: every floor stays the same for everyone | — | more templates (owner's note: think of more typologies later: terraces, mazes, ridge lines…) |
| Which typology | Weights per tower band: floors 1–9 mostly open field, some ruins; from 10 all of them, islands and canyons more common deeper; boss floors favour arenas (crater, mountain). Each stage has a fixed typology: Ruined Gate → ruins, Sunken Crypt → crater, Sky Bastion → islands | — | — |
| Fairness | Every map is checked: every floor cell reachable both ways from the start zone, a walkable 3 × 3 zone, enemies at the minimum distance, climbs within the walking rules; a failed map is redrawn from the same seeded RNG (deterministic), and after the attempts run out the floor falls back to an open field. Holes stay safe (pushes never drop anyone) | — | — |
| Camera and AI | The default zoom fits the board; the zoom range widens a little. The AI's time per action is measured on an 18 × 18 board with five enemies and kept under about 0.3 s | Bigger boards must stay smooth | — |
| Look | Unchanged: the stone board and rocks; props, floor types and biomes stay in milestone 10 | Owner | milestone 10 |

## Outcome
- **Typologies** (`data/maps/typologies/`): open field (2 levels, plateaus), mountain (4 levels, a peak near the centre), crater (3, a rim), islands (62 % land over holes, one-cell bridges laid by `MapShapes.connect_ground`), canyon (a chasm across the middle, 2 crossings), ruins (walls of blocks every 5 cells, a doorway per section, one section in five crumbled). 60–100 % of attempts are playable at every size and layout (20 attempts per map), each under 20 ms.
- **Bands**: floors 1–9 9–11 cells, open field 5 : ruins 2; 10–24 12–15, all six (open field and ruins favoured), boss floors crater and mountain; 25–39 12–15, all six evenly, bosses also on islands; from 40 15–18, islands and canyons favoured. Stages: Ruined Gate on ruins, Sunken Crypt in a crater, Sky Bastion on islands.
- **Approach**: enemies spawn 5–9 MP from the start zone both ways (their walk and the heroes', which differ on slopes; `max_enemy_distance` 9), in the layout's direction (CORNER: along the diagonal toward the far corner), spread apart.
- **Sizes in practice**: an ambush start (from floor 6) uses a map of at least 13 × 13; a map that fails every attempt falls back to a 9 × 9 open field.
- **Fingerprints**: a test pins the exact maps of 13 floors (every typology and layout) and the 3 stages, so a change that alters every floor is made on purpose.
- **Camera**: the starting zoom fits the board (0.85 per cell of the longest side, never below the old default; zoom range up to 28), aimed a third of the way from the centre to the start zone.
- **AI speed**: on 15–18-cell boards with five enemies, 20–24 ms per decision on average, 280 ms at worst (was 370 ms before targeting scanned only a spell's reach).
- **Balance check** (10 AI climbs from floor 1 at level 1): all clear floor 10 (with 9a's maps: 9 of 10).
