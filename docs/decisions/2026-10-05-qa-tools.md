# QA tools (milestone 9c) — decisions

Settled with the owner in one round, after a review of the owner's ideas (a map visualizer, a playground, default teams, auto-battle) plus two suggestions (battle cheats, profile tools). Builds on milestone 9b.

| Decision | Choice | Why | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where | **In the game**, not the editor: the owner playtests in the game, the web build included. A **QA tools** switch in the settings (off by default) shows a **QA** button on the title screen and enables Auto and the battle cheats | One place, every build; it's a beta | release (hide or strip the switch) |
| Saves | **QA battles never touch the save**: no XP, runes, achievements or run progress; their end goes back to the QA screen. Only the profile tools change the save, each after a confirmation | Testing must not spoil real progress | — |
| Floor browser | Any floor number (Previous / Next): its map drawn from above, its shape, size, layout, team composition, enemies, levels and presets; **Fight it** plays that exact floor with the quick team | See and play any floor | — |
| Playground | The board: a floor's own map, or a typology with a seed and a size; the units: up to three heroes (level each) and up to six enemies (level and preset: normal, elite, boss) from lists; they take the map's spawns, and the usual placement phase lets the heroes be moved. No cell-by-cell placement in this version | The most useful tool at a small cost | cell-by-cell placement is wanted |
| Quick team | The three heroes at a chosen level with a rune set of a chosen rarity (none, common, rare, epic, legendary: up to six distinct runes of it) and the default loadout; used by Fight it and as the playground's default | One click to a sensible team | — |
| Auto-battle | An **Auto** toggle in battle (with QA tools on, in any battle, runs included) hands the heroes to the AI until it is turned off; not during the tutorial | Watch the AI, skim floors | — |
| Battle cheats | QA battles only: win now, lose now, kill the pinned unit, heal the living heroes, refill the acting hero's AP and MP; on the player's turn | Reach result screens and edge cases in seconds | — |
| Profile tools | Set every hero's level, give one of every rune, set the best floor, clear every stage, start a tower run at any floor; each asks first, then saves | Test progression without replaying it | — |

## Outcome
- **Switch**: `Settings.qa_tools` (stored in `settings.cfg`), a row in the settings; the title shows a **QA** button when it is on.
- **QA screen** (`scenes/game/qa_screen.tscn`, tabs built in code): Floors (a `MapPreview` drawn from above, the floor's description from `QaTools.describe`: shape, size, layout, composition, enemies with roles, levels and presets), Playground (board from a floor or a typology, seed, size; six enemy rows) and Profile; its choices are kept between visits.
- **QA battles**: `Game.start_qa_battle()` plays the battle screen with `qa_battle` set and goes back to the QA screen on its end or Menu; nothing is applied or saved (a test compares the save file before and after).
- **Auto**: a HUD toggle; EnemyAI plays the heroes (it is team-agnostic, as in the balance lab); switched off, the player takes over at the next decision. It resets with each battle.
- **Cheats**: `Battle.qa_set_hp()` sets a unit's HP and reports it like any change (damage or heal, deaths, the end, the next turn when the acting unit falls); refill restores AP and MP and the free-move budget.
