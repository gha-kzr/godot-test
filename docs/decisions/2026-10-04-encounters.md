# Encounters (milestone 9a) — decisions

Settled with the owner in two rounds. Milestone 9 is split: **9a** (this one) gives enemies roles, team compositions and positioning; **9b** makes maps bigger and adds map typologies from noise (mountains, holes, islands and bridges…, pulled forward from milestone 10), with its own grill.

| Decision | Choice | Why | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scope | Roles, compositions, positioning AI, enemy movement spells, four new enemies, the first stage gated by floor 10 | Roles first, so 9b's bigger maps are filled with meaningful teams | — |
| Roles | **Tank, Bruiser, Ranged, Support, Skirmisher** (a summoner later). Brute and Ghoul: Bruiser; Skeleton Archer: Ranged; Ghost: Skirmisher | Enough variety for compositions without a summoning system | a summoner is wanted |

About ten at first; the Ghost's cap per floor still applies | Teams that make tactical sense (a Witch behind a Shieldbearer) | — |
| Role in the UI | A role tag on the unit card ("Tank", "Support"…) with a tooltip on how the role behaves; nothing on the turn chips | The chips are full | — |

## Outcome (numbers in the data)
- **New enemies** (Quaternius CC0 models): **Orc Guard** (Orc; Tank: 60 HP, 2 MP, physical +30 %; Club 5–7, Shield Bash 6–9 + push 1 every 2 turns, Shield Wall: allies around it −30 % damage for a turn, every 2 turns), **Mushroom Sage** (Mushroom King; Support: 26 HP; Spore Mend heals an ally 7–10, Spore Hex marks a hero +20 % damage taken, Spore Bolt 4–6), **Warg** (Husky; a charger: 5 MP; Pounce 7–9 charging 2–3 cells every 2 turns, Bite 6–8), **Yeti** (a controller: 55 HP, frost +50 %; Icy Grasp 5–7 frost and a 2-cell pull every 3 turns, Smash). The Shieldbearer, Witch and Ogre of the plan became the Orc Guard, the Mushroom Sage and the Yeti, the closest models available in the heroes' cartoon style; the Warg is a low-poly husky.
- **Positioning**: Ranged and Support stay 3–4 walking steps from the nearest hero (the Support also near an ally); the Tank 1–2, next to an ally; Bruisers and the Skirmisher have none (straight at the heroes). Kiting guard tests: a melee hero catches the Skeleton Archer down a 40-cell corridor (the test fails with a 7–8 band), and AI heroes at level 10 beat the Fortress team (two ranged, a tank, a support) at floor 27.
- **Compositions**: sixteen; floors 1–4: a lone brute, raiders (bruiser + ranged), brawl; 5–9: two-enemy teams; 10–39: shield line, wolf pack, skirmish, haunt (3); 40–79: warband, fortress, haunted band (4); from 80: horde, siege (5); boss teams of 2 to 5. Balance check (10 AI climbs from floor 1 at level 1): 9 clear floor 10 (before: 8 of 10).

- **Review fixes** (during 9c): the positioning distance is what the nearest hero must walk to reach a cell (it was the unit's walk to the hero: on slopes an archer on high ground thought itself catchable when it wasn't; a kiting test from high ground guards it), and a cast is scored against where it leaves the heroes. The Tank's "between an ally and the heroes" is in practice its 1–2 band plus the ally bonus; the plan's per-role AI profiles became a `Positioning` on each enemy. Validation now reports a `max_distance` past a hero's reach (4), a composition with more than 6 slots or more fixed copies of an enemy than its `max_per_floor`, and a weight below 1.
