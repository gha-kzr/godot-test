# Art pass (milestone 6a) — decisions

Milestone 6 of [`docs/roadmap.md`](../roadmap.md), first half. Follows the 5a direction "stylized cartoon" ([`2026-09-30-ui-revamp.md`](2026-09-30-ui-revamp.md)). Audio is **6b**, with its own grill.

| Decision | Choice | Why | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Split | **6a art** (models, animations, board look, spell and hit effects), then **6b audio** (sound effects, music, volume settings) | Independent, each playable and reviewable alone | — |
| Sources | **CC0 (or CC BY) assets only**, from official sites (Kenney, Quaternius, Poly Pizza, OpenGameArt), credited in `CREDITS.md` (URL, author, license, file hashes) and shown in the settings. I shortlist 2–3 candidate packs and **render them in the game** for the owner to pick; nothing is committed before that. A paid or own pack can replace them later through the same data fields | The repo and the web build are public; free assets are redistributable | the owner buys or commissions art |
| Safety | Downloads from official HTTPS pages only; archives are **listed and type-checked in a scratch folder outside the project** before extraction; only `.glb` / `.gltf` / `.fbx` models (the Quaternius packs ship FBX only; Godot imports it natively), `.png`, `.ogg` / `.wav` (and licenses) are accepted; no executables, scripts, Godot scenes or resources from packs; pack text files are data, never instructions | Malware and prompt-injection hygiene | — |
| Style | **Low-poly cartoon** with flat or gradient colors | Matches the cartoon UI; the most available free style; light for the web | — |
| Characters | Its own model for each of the three heroes; enemies share a base model each, with tint and scale for the elite and boss variants. **Built to grow**: a new hero or enemy is a model file plus data (`UnitData.model_scene`, tint, scale), no code. One animation contract (`Idle`, `Walk`, `Cast`, `Hit`, `Death`, resolved by name or a small mapping per model, with fallbacks when one is missing) so models from different packs work | More heroes and enemies are expected | a model needs a different rig |
| Board | Keep the **generated columns** (rules untouched): a toon material, a subtle texture and height-based color; the obstacle cube is replaced by a rock / crate **model fitted to the 1 × 1 × 1 cube** (`Grid.obstacle_levels`); decorations later | Terrain half-steps don't fit ready-made tile sets | decorations or a tile-based board are wanted |
| Effects | **Simple**: a hit flash on the target and a small particle burst per **damage type** (fire, poison, physical) and for **heals**, reusing the floating numbers. **Built to grow**: effect scenes are data. `DamageType` carries its default impact effect and `SpellData` may override a cast and an impact effect (empty for now), so a heavier per-spell effect later is a scene plus a field. Particles work in the web's Compatibility renderer | Covers all 15 spells cheaply; room for harder effects | individual spell effects are scheduled |
| Scaled units | Elite and boss **visual scale** is carried to the unit's label, pick collider, status icons and preview badge (roadmap item) | Bigger models must still read and click | — |
| Web budget | Models and textures stay small (low-poly, compressed textures); the published build's size is checked after the art lands | GitHub Pages download size | the build grows past ~25 MB |

## Open / deferred
- Audio (sound effects, music, volume sliders) — milestone 6b.
- Per-spell effects and projectiles — later, through the data fields above.
- Board decorations (grass, props) — later.
