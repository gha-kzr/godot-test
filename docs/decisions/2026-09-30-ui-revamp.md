# UI and UX revamp (milestone 5) — decisions

Milestone 5 of [`docs/roadmap.md`](../roadmap.md). Replaces the first-version battle HUD and screens recorded as "to be revamped in M5" in [`2026-09-28-status-effects.md`](2026-09-28-status-effects.md) (status display, unit inspection, HUD refresh), [`2026-09-28-progression.md`](2026-09-28-progression.md) (party screen), [`2026-09-29-design-tools.md`](2026-09-29-design-tools.md) (enemy labels) and [`2026-09-29-run-loop.md`](2026-09-29-run-loop.md) (hub, run screen, HUD floor label).

| Decision | Choice | Why | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Size | Two milestones: **5a battle** (HUD, timeline, unit cards, status icons, damage preview, move costs, guidance, small battle UX items), then **5b screens** (theme, title, hub, run screen, settings, first-run hints). 5b gets its own grill round | Each half is playable and reviewable alone; the battle is where most time is spent | — |
| Visual direction | **Stylized cartoon** (Dofus / Wakfu): bright colors, rounded panels and buttons, a Godot `Theme` resource | Matches the models M6 will bring | M6 art picks a different style |
| Icons | **game-icons.net** (CC BY 3.0), single-color SVGs tinted per damage type / status; credited in `CREDITS.md` and (5b) in the settings screen | Consistent, free, tintable | own art is commissioned |
| Climbing cost | **Kept** (+1 MP per level climbed) and made readable: the hovered path shows its MP total ("4 MP") and marks climbing steps | Height stays a tactical lever; the label explains every cost | playtests still find it confusing |
| Input and platforms | Desktop mouse and keyboard. Mobile and gamepad are **nice-to-haves, not built**: nothing is hover-only (hover info also opens on click / tap), menus support focus navigation | Keeps the door open at little cost | a mobile or console target is chosen |
| Name | Placeholder **"Tower Tactics"**, only in `project.godot`'s application name | Easy to change | a real name is chosen |
| Battle layout | Turn order at the top, spells bottom centre, the active hero's panel bottom left, the unit card on the right (today's positions, restyled) | Familiar; leaves the board clear | playtests |
| Timeline | Top bar shows the **next 5 turns** only, as **portrait chips with an HP bar**; hovering a chip highlights its unit, clicking moves the camera to it; a **full-order overlay** (all units, both teams, with HP and statuses) opens on a shortcut (`Tab`) and a button | Readable with many units; the full order stays one key away | — |
| Status display | Small **icons with a turns-left number** above each unit's HP bar; full descriptions in the unit cards | Reads at a glance, keeps the countdown | — |
| Unit inspection | Hovering a unit shows its **card**; **clicking pins it**; clicking another unit pins that one; only the card's **✕** or **Esc** unpins (clicking the pinned unit again does nothing) — also the touch path | Fast on desktop; compare or read on a tablet; one simple way out | — |
| Damage preview | While aiming, each unit in the hovered area shows the **expected damage / heal range** ("8–11"), a **skull** when it can kill, and the **status icons** the spell would apply; computed by the battle's own formula | The biggest readability win in tactics games; never wrong | — |
| Guidance | A **persistent prompt line** for the current step ("Place your heroes…", "Choose a target…"); **End turn pulses** when the active hero can neither afford a spell nor move | Covers the placement hints and the "nothing left to do" hint | — |
