# Milestone 9c: QA tools — plan

Decisions: [`docs/decisions/2026-10-05-qa-tools.md`](../decisions/2026-10-05-qa-tools.md). Branch `milestone-9c` (on top of `milestone-9b`); one commit per task; tests for the rules and the flows (no save touched); French texts.

1. **Switch and screen**: `Settings.qa_tools` (stored, a settings row), a **QA** button on the title when on, a `QaScreen` with tabs (Floors, Playground, Profile); `Game.start_qa_battle()`: the battle screen with a QA flag, its end and its Menu back to the QA screen, nothing applied or saved.
2. **Floor browser**: `Encounter` remembers its typology, layout and composition (set by the floor generator); a `MapPreview` control draws a map from above (heights, blocks, holes, spawns); floor number, Previous / Next, the encounter's description, Fight it.
3. **Quick team and playground**: `QaTeams.quick_team(roster, level, rarity)` (records at that level, distinct runes of the rarity, default loadout); the playground's board (a floor's map or a typology, seed and size, generated for the chosen enemy count) and its units (heroes and levels, enemies with level and preset).
4. **Auto-battle and cheats**: an Auto toggle in the HUD (QA tools on, no tutorial) that lets EnemyAI play the heroes; in QA battles a cheat bar: `Battle.qa_set_hp()` (events and deaths through the usual reporting) for win, lose, kill and heal; refill AP and MP.
5. **Profile tools**: set levels, every rune, best floor, clear stages, start a run at a floor; a confirm step each; saved.
6. **Docs, translations, review.**
