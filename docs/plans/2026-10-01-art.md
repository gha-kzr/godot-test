# Art pass (milestone 6a) — implementation plan

Decisions: [`docs/decisions/2026-10-01-art.md`](../decisions/2026-10-01-art.md). Roadmap: milestone 6.
Architecture: assets are data. Models through `UnitData.model_scene` (wrapped by a small `UnitModel` animation contract), the board through `BoardTheme`, effects through scenes referenced from `DamageType` / `SpellData`. No rule changes; the event stream gains the damage type so views can pick the effect.

## Design summary
- `assets/<pack>/…` per source pack, each with its license file; `CREDITS.md` rows with URL, author, license and SHA-256 of the files used.
- `UnitModel` (script on the model root, or a wrapper scene): finds the `AnimationPlayer`, plays `Idle` on loop, `play(name)` with fallbacks; `UnitView` calls it from `play_move` / `play_cast` / `play_hit` / `play_death` and awaits the length.
- Tint and scale per unit: `UnitData.color` and `visual_scale` (presets) applied to the model's materials; the scale also reaches the label, collider, status row and preview badge.
- Board: `BoardTheme` toon material (`ShaderMaterial` or `StandardMaterial3D` with toon settings that work in Compatibility), subtle texture, obstacle model from the pack fitted to `Grid.obstacle_levels × level_height`.
- Effects: `BattleEvents.DamageDealt` carries its `damage_type`; `DamageType.impact_effect: PackedScene`, `SpellData.cast_effect` / `impact_effect` overrides, a heal effect in a small `BattleFx` resource; `CPUParticles3D` scenes (web-safe), spawned and freed by the `EventPlayer` / `UnitView`.

## Tasks
Each task ends with: `--check-only`, headless tests green (plus mutation checks), `tools/fill_uid_refs.gd`, a render check for visual changes, a commit of explicit paths. Assets only enter the repo after the owner's pick.

- [x] **Task 1: Shortlist and pick the packs** — 2–3 candidate CC0 packs for characters (with animations), obstacle / board props and particles textures; downloaded and inspected in a scratch folder, rendered in the game through a throwaway scene, shown to the owner. Chosen files copied to `assets/`, credited.
  Pattern: a safe intake routine (list, type-check, hash, review) before import; glTF imported by Godot as data.
  Done: candidates rendered in a scratch project (Kenney Blocky Characters, Mini Dungeon, Mini Arena; Quaternius Ultimate Animated Character Pack and Modular Dungeon Pack); the owner chose **Quaternius** (downloaded by the owner from the pack pages, scanned: only `.fbx`, real FBX headers, no symlinks or odd paths). All characters share one 32-bone skeleton and 17 animations (`Idle`, `Walk`, `Run`, `SwordSlash`, `Shoot_OneHanded`, `Punch`, `RecieveHit`, `Death`, `Victory`, `Defeat`…, names prefixed `CharacterArmature|`). The pack's `Skin` material is a near-black gray: skin color becomes a data field. Copied to `assets/quaternius/` (Knight_Male, Wizard, Elf, Goblin_Male, Viking_Male; Barrel, Chest, Rock1-3) with `SOURCE.md` (page URLs, archive and file SHA-256); credited in `CREDITS.md`. The settings credits now show only the credit rows (asset, author, license), not the contributor prose. Roster: Knight → Knight_Male, Mage → Wizard, Ranger → Elf, Archer → Goblin_Male, Brute → Viking_Male.
- [x] **Task 2: Unit models and animations** — `UnitModel` contract, hero and enemy models, tint and scale, scaled label / collider / tags.
  Pattern: `AnimationPlayer` with name-based animations and fallbacks; data-driven `model_scene`.
  Done: `UnitModel` (`scripts/view/unit_model.gd`): instances a pack model, finds its `AnimationPlayer`, resolves logical names (`Idle`, `Walk`, `Attack`, `Cast`, `Hit`, `Death`, `Victory`, `Defeat`) to the model's own names by candidate list and the part after a `|` (a missing one is absent, `play` returns its length or 0), loops Idle / Walk, re-colors the `Skin` material on a per-instance mesh copy, and flashes (material overlay). `UnitData` gains `model_scale`, `model_height` and `skin_color`; `SpellData.cast_animation` (empty: Attack for range 1, Cast otherwise). `UnitView` plays Walk while moving, Attack / Cast on a cast (waiting at most 0.7 s for a longer animation), Hit + flash on damage, Death before the shrink; the HP label, status icons, damage preview and click capsule sit above the model's height **times the elite / boss scale** (the roadmap's scaled-units item); units start facing the nearest enemy. The five units carry their Quaternius models (Knight_Male, Wizard, Elf, Goblin_Male, Viking_Male).
- [x] **Task 3: Board look** — toon material, texture, height color, obstacle model fitted to the cube.
  Pattern: `BoardTheme` resource fields; materials shared per color.
  Done: `BoardTheme` gains `toon` (toon diffuse, no specular: works in the Compatibility renderer) and `noise_strength` (a 16 × 16 speckle texture, nearest filter, generated with a fixed seed so there is no texture asset), and `obstacle_scenes` (replacing the single `obstacle_scene`): one model per obstacle cell, picked by cell, **stretched to the cube exactly** (`BoardView._fitted_model`, `model_bounds`), so the drawn rock is as tall as what blocks sight. `data/board/stone.tres` uses Quaternius Rock1-3 and is the battle scene's theme; a theme without models keeps the cubes. The rocks are flat (about 1 × 0.4 × 1.1) and read as standing stones after the stretch: the Column or other props can be added to the list, or fitted another way, if that look isn't liked. Terrain height colors (`lighten_per_level`) and the highlights are unchanged.
- [x] **Task 4: Effects** — damage type on the event, effect scenes per damage type and heal, hit flash, data override fields.
  Pattern: data-referenced `PackedScene` effects spawned by the view layer from events; `CPUParticles3D`.
  Done: `BattleEvents.DamageDealt.damage_type`; `Fx` (`scripts/view/fx.gd`: a one-shot scene that starts its `CPUParticles3D` children and frees itself after `duration`); scenes `scenes/fx/` (physical sparks, fire, poison, heal, a generic hit); `DamageType.impact_effect` (the three shipped types set), `SpellData.cast_effect` / `impact_effect` (empty: the per-spell overrides for later), `BattleFx` (`data/fx/battle_fx.tres`: default impact and heal, `impact_for(type, spell)`: spell, else type, else default). `EventPlayer` tracks the spell of the current cast (cleared by a turn event or status tick) and spawns the effect on the target (`UnitView.spawn_fx`, at chest height, scaled with the unit); none for a fully resisted hit or a zero heal. The hit flash and floating numbers are unchanged.
- [x] **Task 5: Web check, review and docs** — export size and a browser look at the build, `godot-code-review` subagent, README, roadmap (6a done, 6b next), credits complete.
  Done: the web export (single-threaded) grew by about 1.3 MB for all the art (pack 2.0 MB, 1.8 MB gzipped; the engine's wasm is about 10 MB gzipped and dominates), the exported pack holds the models with their 17 animations, the rocks and the effects, and boots headless. A `godot-code-review` subagent reviewed `main..HEAD`. Fixed, each with a test: `UnitModel.reset()` (a synced, revived unit stands again: no held Death pose or flash); `_fitted_model` keeps a model root's own origin; the hit animation waits for its length and a zero-damage hit changes nothing; obstacle variants use `hash(cell)` (no diagonal stripes); one shared speckle texture; `Fx` frees itself on a tween (pauses with the tree) and a test checks every shipped effect's duration covers its particles; the credits screen shows bullets and the source column too (CC BY attribution: author, license and source name); FBX recorded as an accepted format. Kept as is (with the reason in the code): the skin uses a mesh copy rather than a surface override, because freeing an override material logs an engine error in the headless renderer. Also fixed (playtest note): the inspect card goes to the screen edge away from the unit it shows, so hovering an enemy at the top right no longer puts the card on top of it (`Hud.set_inspect_side`, `BattleController._unit_is_right_of_center`).
- [x] **Playtest fixes (effects and pacing)** — four notes from playing the branch:
  - **Forced pause**: the model animations had made each cast, hit and death hold the event queue (up to 0.7 s / 0.9 s), so clicks during them were ignored (this was also the "second spell quickly does nothing" report). Actions no longer hold the queue: a cast waits only its lunge (0.3 s), a hit its flash (0.3 s), a death 0.5 s plus a 0.25 s shrink; the animation plays on by itself and returns to Idle when it ends (`UnitModel` listens to `animation_finished`), or the next action replaces it. Pacing is back to about 0.85 s per damaging cast.
  - **Repeat casts**: asking an `AnimationPlayer` for the animation it is already playing does nothing (verified), so `UnitModel.play` now restarts an action (seek to 0) when it is asked again.
  - **Nothing at all on some spells**: effects now also play for a fully resisted hit and a heal on a unit at full HP (the spell landed); a spell cast at an empty cell shows a ground effect on every cell of its area with no unit (`BattleFx.cell_effect_for`: the spell's own, else its typed damage's, else the heal effect, else the default; `EventPlayer._spawn_ground_fx`); a status gets a burst tinted with its color (`BattleFx.status_applied`, `scenes/fx/status.tscn`, `Fx.set_tint`).
  - **Buffs and heals** are cast, not swung: a spell with no damage effect uses `Cast` even at range 1.
