# Milestone 8: hero depth — plan

Decisions: [`docs/decisions/2026-10-03-hero-depth.md`](../decisions/2026-10-03-hero-depth.md). Branch `milestone-8`; one commit per task; tests in the battle layer first (no nodes), then views; `godot-code-review` at the end; new texts through `tr()` with French entries (`tools/extract_strings.gd`). The game is in beta: no save migrations.

1. **Repositioning**: `UnitState.segment_cell` / `segment_mp` (set at turn start and after every cast: `commit_position()`); `Movement.reach()` floods from the segment's start with its MP (the standing cell is excluded, the start cell is reachable again); `Move` sets MP to `segment_mp − cost`, and its event carries the walked path from the standing cell (`Movement.walk_path`, unlimited MP; a slide when no walk exists) and the MP after; HUD model and path preview follow. AI: positions only while it hasn't moved this segment. Pattern: the existing command actions over a state snapshot (the "segment" is the snapshot undo would have used).
2. **Cooldowns**: `SpellData.cooldown`; `UnitState.cooldowns` (spell → turns left, counted down at the caster's turn start, cloned); `CastSpell` validates and sets it; `can_afford` (shared with the UI) and the AI respect it; the spell bar shows the turns left on a slot; spell descriptions name it.
3. **Movement effects**: `MoveEffect` (`EffectData` strategy subclass) with kinds TELEPORT, JUMP, CHARGE, PUSH, PULL, RETREAT and a `distance`; effects get the cast's target cell through a new `apply_cast()` hook (default: `apply()`), and can veto a target cell (`allows_target()`, used by `Targeting.can_target`, so the highlights, the AI and validation agree). A `UnitDisplaced` event (from, to, kind); the view animates it (blink, arc, dash, slide) through `UnitView`. Damage preview ignores it.
4. **Resistance caps**: 75 % heroes, 100 % enemies (`UnitState.max_resistance_percent()`); the card says "immune" at 100 %.
5. **Loadout**: `HeroRecord.loadout` (5 slots, null at the end only), `known_spells()`, `normalize_loadout()` (drops unknown, fills empty slots in learning order), `assign_spell(slot, spell)` (swap or replace); `HeroData` milestone rewards may unlock spells; `MAX_SPELLS` gone (known spells unlimited, 5 active); the profile saves loadouts; `Profile.set_loadout_spell()` and a `loadout_requested` signal through `HeroPanel` → `PartyScreen` → `Game` (saved like a rune change). Party screen: five slot buttons, then the inactive known spells; click a slot, then a spell.
6. **Content: hero spells**: Holy and Frost damage types (with impact effects tinted from the existing ones), statuses (Marked, Chilled for Frost Lance), nine spells with icons (game-icons.net, credited), level rewards at 5, 9 and 14.
7. **Content: enemies**: the three models (downloaded from poly.pizza, hashes in `assets/quaternius/SOURCE.md`, credits), the animation-name lookup extended, units, enemies, loot, statuses (Dread for Wail), tower pools and the slice encounter without the Archer; a Ghost cap per floor (`EnemyData.max_per_floor`), no Ghost in boss pools; delete the Archer.
8. **Docs, translations and review**: README (playing, adding content), roadmap and backlog, French texts, `godot-code-review`, renders.
