# Milestone 9b: bigger maps and map typologies — plan

Decisions: [`docs/decisions/2026-10-04-maps.md`](../decisions/2026-10-04-maps.md). Branch `milestone-9b` (on top of `milestone-9a`); one commit per task; generator tests over many seeds; renders through the battle camera; `godot-code-review` at the end.

1. **Bounded approach**: enemy spawns are chosen among the cells at a walking distance of `min_enemy_distance` to `max_enemy_distance` (new, about 9) MP from the start zone, in the layout's direction (in front for EDGE, toward the far corner for CORNER, all around for AMBUSH), spread apart (farthest-point picking); the size no longer sets the approach.
2. **Sizes per band**: `FloorBand.map_min_size` / `map_max_size` (0: the settings'), boss floors +2, capped at 20; the bands get 9–11, 12–15 and 15–18.
3. **Typologies**: a `MapTypology` resource (kind, heights, noise frequency, obstacle and hole densities) and one height-field function per kind (open field, mountain, crater, islands with bridges, canyon with crossings, ruins); `FastNoiseLite` seeded from the floor's RNG; the zone and spawns placed on the result; the playability check and redraws as today, the open-field fallback last. `FloorBand.map_typologies` / `boss_map_typologies` (weights), `StageData.map_typology`.
4. **Camera**: the default zoom fits the board; a slightly wider zoom range.
5. **AI speed**: measured on an 18 × 18 board with five enemies; trimmed if needed.
6. **Docs, review, a light balance check** (one tower run from floor 1).
